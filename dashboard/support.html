Support & FAQ

How do I share my child's logs with another parent/caregiver?
On the Apple Watch app, tap the "Share" button to generate an iCloud Share link. Send this link to the other person, and they can enter it on the GoTime Dashboard to view the logs.
Is my data secure?
Yes! All data is securely stored in your personal iCloud account using Apple CloudKit. We do not have access to your data.
I found a bug or need a new feature, how can I contact you?
We track all issues, bug reports, and feature requests on GitHub.

👉 Open an Issue on GitHub
How do I delete my data?
Since GoTime uses your personal iCloud database, you can manage and delete your app data directly through your iOS/watchOS device settings under iCloud Storage.
