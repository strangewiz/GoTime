Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 10, 2026

1. Data Collection

GoTime: Bathroom Tracker collects no data beyond what you explicitly enter into the app. All your log records are stored securely in your private iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit. We do not maintain any independent servers or databases for user data.

2. Third-Party Analytics

We do not use any third-party analytics services. GoTime does not track your usage behavior or app performance metrics using external tools.

3. Advertising

We do not serve advertisements in GoTime and do not include any third-party advertising SDKs.

4. Data Sharing

Your data is never shared with any third parties. Only you, and users you explicitly share your iCloud records with, can access your GoTime logs.
